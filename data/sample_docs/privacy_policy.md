# Privacy Policy Notes

The organisation stores personal data for account management, support requests and service improvement. The main risk is personal data exposure if permissions are configured badly or if old records are kept for longer than necessary.

The policy recommends limiting access to sensitive records, reviewing retention periods and recording where personal information is stored. Staff should be able to identify which systems contain customer information and who is responsible for maintaining them.

The document also explains that clear evidence is needed when answering questions about data protection. A summary should link back to the relevant source section rather than relying on memory or informal notes.
